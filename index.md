I am a postdoctoral researcher at the Geneva School of Economics and Management (University of Geneva) with [Michele Pelizzari](https://sites.google.com/site/mipellizzari/).

I hold a PhD in Economics from Aix-Marseille School of Economics (AMSE) where I was supervised by [Yann Bramoullé](https://sites.google.com/site/bramoulley/)  and [Roberta Ziparo](https://sites.google.com/site/rziparo/).

In my research, I document gender gaps across academic disciplines and examine how competition in male-dominated environments affects women's performance and career trajectories.
